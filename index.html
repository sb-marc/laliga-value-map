Each dot is a player who has appeared this season, plotted by price against Average points per appearance (toggle log/linear price scale on the right — log spreads out the bulk of sub-€5M players for easier comparison; linear preserves true euro distances but crowds them together). The dashed line is a linear regression fit within each position (price scales very differently for a goalkeeper than a forward). Color shows the gap between actual price and what the position's pricing line predicts — blue dots cost less than their output suggests (bargains), red dots cost more (paying for reputation, not output yet).

Method: for each position, Price (€) is regressed on Average points per appearance (OLS, players with ≥1 game played; 80 players with 0 appearances excluded for lack of signal). R² for the pooled model is ~0.40 — price is only partly explained by output this early in the season (7 gameweeks), which is itself a finding: a large share of current pricing still reflects preseason reputation rather than realized production.
